Install CrewCue

iPhones only show pop-up cues for apps on the Home Screen. Do this once:

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow) in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari and open CrewCue from your Home Screen.

Needs iOS 16.4 or newer. Using Chrome on iPhone? Open this link in Safari instead.
